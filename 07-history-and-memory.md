## Persistent context for future work

The approved product boundary is deterministic decision management/delivery/execution. Keep proposed extensions distinguishable. Preserve tiered approval, maker exclusion, immutable artifacts, exact pinning, atomic execution snapshots, Shadow isolation, sticky Canary, two-second convergence, sensitive-data protection and retention ranges.

For new diagram work, start from the matching source family and the user conventions in [diagram knowledge](06-diagrams-and-conventions.md). Do not map `SRS:UC-*` to an older proposal's same-numbered UC by name alone. Separate high-level goals from internal reused behavior and separate governance from deployment.
